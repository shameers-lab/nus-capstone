# Phase 1 Dashboard Story

## Business problem
The dataset tracks traffic volume on westbound I-94 near Minneapolis–St Paul. The key business question is to understand which temporal and weather conditions are associated with higher traffic volumes and to identify the main congestion patterns.

## Key dashboard visuals
1. Average traffic volume by hour of day
2. Average traffic volume by day of week
3. Average traffic volume by weather condition
4. Temperature vs traffic volume
5. Holiday vs non-holiday comparison

## Insights to communicate
- Traffic volume is highest during the busiest commuter periods, reflecting predictable rush-hour patterns.
- Weekday patterns show stronger travel activity than weekends, which is typical for commuter traffic.
- Weather has a measurable relationship with traffic volume, with rain conditions increasing the likelihood of heavier traffic relative to clear conditions.
- Temperature has a weak but positive relationship with traffic volume, indicating that travel behaviour is influenced by seasonal and environmental conditions.
- Holiday differences are present but comparatively smaller than the effect of time of day and weather in this dataset.

## Dashboard interpretation
The dashboard should support a practical narrative: the traffic network is primarily shaped by time-of-day travel demand, then modified by weather and occasional holiday effects. This makes the business case for traffic forecasting and congestion management clear.

## Report use
These findings provide the foundation for later project phases, including feature engineering, forecasting, ML modeling, and decision-support outputs.
